# Synthetic demonstration outputs

The event-study coefficients and plot in this folder come from synthetic data. They illustrate a workflow and have no substantive interpretation.

The real-data September 2026 exploration is stored separately in [research/2026-09-20](../research/2026-09-20/README.md).

## Identification and current scale

A causal interpretation of the proposed differential-exposure event study would require comparable counterfactual trends and other design assumptions. Pre-period coefficients are diagnostics: failure to reject a pre-trend does not establish parallel trends. Selecting a window after seeing results does not repair identification.

The two-sector policy pilot is insufficient for reliable sector-clustered inference. Existing patent differences are descriptive and require source auditing. See the [revised design](../researchstrategy_ChinaWTO.md).
