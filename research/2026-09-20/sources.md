# Sources and attribution

Retrieved or checked 20 September 2026. Estimates and interpretation in the accompanying report are new exploratory calculations, not published findings by the dataset authors.

## Data actually used

1. Hanson, Jonathan K., and Rachel Sigman (2021). *Leviathan's Latent Dimensions: Measuring State Capacity for Comparative Political Research*. Journal of Politics 83(4), 1495–1510. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1086/715066). State Capacity Dataset v1, 1960–2015. [Release and documentation](https://websites.umich.edu/~jkhanson/state_capacity.html).
2. Acemoglu, Daron, Suresh Naidu, Pascual Restrepo, and James A. Robinson (2019). *Democracy Does Cause Growth*. Journal of Political Economy 127(1), 47–100. [Paper](https://www.nber.org/papers/w20004). [Original replication files](https://knowledge.uchicago.edu/records/de4b6-9qv06). The present merge does not reproduce the paper's causal estimates.
3. Bolt, Jutta, and Jan Luiten van Zanden. *Maddison-style estimates of the evolution of the world economy: A new 2023 update*. Published online 2024; Journal of Economic Surveys 39(2), 2025, 631–671. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/joes.12618). [Maddison Project Database 2023](https://doi.org/10.34894/INZBF2), version 1.0, release 26 April 2024, data through 2022. [Release notes and attribution](https://www.rug.nl/ggdc/historicaldevelopment/maddison/releases/maddison-project-database-2023?lang=en). Data licensed CC BY 4.0; the report transforms the data into growth measures and regressions. Underlying sources from the release workbook are retained below.
4. World Bank, *World Development Indicators*, NY.GDP.PCAP.KD, GDP per capita (constant 2015 US$). [Indicator](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/NY.GDP.PCAP.KD). API lastupdated 2026-07-13; requested years 1960–2024. This diagnostic was added after the initial GDP sources disagreed.
5. Wenwen Zhang, [state-coordination repository](https://github.com/wzhang109/state-coordination), snapshot `0346cc0`. Existing patent counts were only internally recomputed; the upstream query and sector mapping were not independently rerun or verified.
